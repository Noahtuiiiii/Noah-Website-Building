05 / Off the clock

The person behind the plans.

Operating principle

I'm suspicious of complexity that doesn't earn its keep.

The best plan is the simplest one that still survives contact with reality. I'd rather cut a slide than add one, and I think most status meetings could be a well-written paragraph. Clarity is a kindness — to the team, and to whoever has to read the report at 9pm.

Outside work that instinct shows up everywhere: I keep a too-long list of books, a too-short list of finished side projects, and strong opinions about both.
